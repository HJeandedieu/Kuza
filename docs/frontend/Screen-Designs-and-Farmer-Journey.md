# Kuza Screen Designs and Farmer Journey

**Owner:** Ishimwe Jenny Miriotta  
**Status:** Draft for team review

Design the phone layout first. Add a wireframe link for each screen as the designs are prepared.

| Screen | What the farmer does | What the screen shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign in and registration | Create an account or return to a field. | Clear labels and a way to correct invalid entries. |
| Field list and setup | Open a field or add maize, location, planting date or crop age, and current condition. | Saved fields, an empty state for new users, and confirmation after saving. |
| Field dashboard | See what needs attention and why. | Growth stage, weekly tasks, advice with its source, and weather and sensor status. |
| Plant review | Answer symptom questions and optionally share a photo with consent. | Photo preview, upload progress, result, and a clear next step for uncertain or failed assessments. |
| Harvest and storage | Find advice as the crop reaches the relevant stage. | The recommended action, why it applies, and its source. |

Review the flow on a small phone screen. Ask a reviewer to add a field and explain its next task without prompting. Use readable text, clear field labels and status messages that work without relying on color.
