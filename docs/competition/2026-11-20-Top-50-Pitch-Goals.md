## What we want judges to see

Show a software prototype that helps a maize farmer decide whether to irrigate and whether to apply fertilizer through irrigation. Use simulated field and weather data for both recommendations and explain why Kuza gives each one. Eunice verifies the fertigation example before rehearsal. The field is still wet and the sample forecast shows rain. Show the proposed advice to wait, explain the two inputs, and point to the source of the advice. Label the field, forecast and sensor reading as samples wherever they are simulated. Aim for **30–45 seconds** for this example.
